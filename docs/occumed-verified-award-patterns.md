# Verified Occu-Med award and performance patterns

Last researched: 2026-08-08

## Purpose

This file records public evidence of work Occu-Med has actually won or publicly documented performance it has delivered. Ultra Search uses these records as **similarity evidence** for the scope and buyer language of future opportunities.

They are **not live opportunities**. An award notice, closed solicitation, expired bid, or historical contract must still be rejected by the lifecycle gate even when its scope is an excellent Occu-Med match.

## What this teaches Ultra Search

The strongest repeated win patterns are:

1. **Occupational-health / employee medical examinations** — pre-employment, pre-placement, periodic, exit/termination, return-to-work/return-to-duty and fitness-for-duty.
2. **Medical surveillance** — often recurring BPAs/IDIQs and often paired with lab services, audiometry, spirometry and reporting.
3. **Distributed delivery** — nationwide exam locations, clinics throughout a state, or multiple government locations. A requirement does not become irrelevant merely because no single clinic can cover the whole footprint.
4. **Public-safety / regulated workforces** — law enforcement, firefighters, National Guard, USACE operations personnel and other safety-sensitive employees.
5. **Program administration / medical review** — fitness determinations, medical review/consultation, record/case review, results guidance and reporting are legitimate parts of the scope.
6. **Government-contractor / overseas workforce screening** — fit-for-duty and readiness screening for defense-prime and deployed/base-operations work is a proven delivery pattern.
7. **Ancillary exam components are additive, not disqualifying** — labs, audiometry, spirometry, EKG, vision/hearing and similar components commonly appear inside otherwise excellent-fit occupational-health contracts.

## Guardrail

Historical similarity evidence can increase confidence that an **open** solicitation is within Occu-Med's demonstrated service model. It must never override evidence that the record itself is awarded, expired, closed, cancelled, dead, or otherwise not currently pursuable.
